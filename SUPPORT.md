# Support

## Community support

Use the public community channels for Community Edition:

- [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/everstacklabs/everstack/discussions)
  for setup help, architecture questions, and ideas.
- [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/everstacklabs/everstack/issues) for
  reproducible bugs and accepted feature work.
- [Documentation](https://docs.everstack.ai) for product and API guides.

Please include your Everstack version, deployment method, operating system,
relevant configuration with secrets removed, and a minimal reproduction.
Community support is best-effort and has no response-time SLA.

## Security reports

Do not report vulnerabilities in a public issue. Follow
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) and email
[security@everstack.ai](mailto:security@everstack.ai).

## Commercial support

Managed service, onboarding, deployment assistance, and contractual support are
available separately. Contact [support@everstack.ai](mailto:support@everstack.ai)
or [sales@everstack.ai](mailto:sales@everstack.ai).
